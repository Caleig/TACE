[h1]Thorium Accessory Expansion[/h1]

An add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2909886416]Thorium[/url] that expands accessory progression across multiple classes. Most of the current content focuses on the Healer's dark branch, alongside accessories for other classes.

[h2]General[/h2]
[list]
[*]Foxfire Spirit Orbs: four extra jumps, faster acceleration, no fall damage, and a spirit-flame dash.
[/list]

[h2]Healer[/h2]
[list]
[*]Cursed Covenant: empowers radiant attacks at the cost of life while building Fallen Charge.
[*]Bone Covenant: trades healing for a higher radiant damage cap.
[*]Fallen Covenant: higher life costs and radiant damage, with a critical strike bonus at full charge.
[*]Sin of Heresy: greatly boosts radiant damage and critical strike chance, and amplifies Shadowflame and Light Curse.
[*]Karmic Covenant: your healing streak fuels a crucible for lasting radiant damage, and healing is shared with allies.
[*]Eternal Bulwark: absorbs damage for teammates and forms a protective bubble at low life.
[*]Michael's Aid: bonus healing grants radiant bonuses, and your healing becomes more efficient.
[/list]

[h2]Scythe Accessories[/h2]
[list]
[*]Soul of the Scythe: spinning a scythe hurls phantom copies at your cursor.
[*]Spirit Fire Scythe: phantom kills release life spirits that heal allies.
[*]Anubis: phantom hits inflict Holy Glare, and phantom kills restore life.
[*]Styx Blood Ankh: Blood Charge for double-damage hits, and Blood Boost for nearby allies.
[*]Radiant Holy Ankh: phantoms generate soul essence and protect nearby allies.
[/list]

[h2]Magic Sheaths[/h2]
[list]
[*]Mana Sheath: spending mana conjures spellblades whose hits restore mana.
[*]Champion's Spellblade Sheath: more spellblades and extra magic damage.
[*]Terra Spellblade Sheath: homing spellblades that grant damage and defense.
[*]Champion's Broken Blade: more magic damage and piercing spellblades.
[*]Blazing Blade Pact: spellblade hits explode and set enemies ablaze.
[*]Energy Blade Pact: spellblades inflict Granite Surge, increasing damage taken.
[*]Lodestone Blade Pact: spellblades inflict Sundered, increasing damage taken.
[*]Holy Blade Pact: spellblades inflict Holy Glare, increasing damage taken.
[*]Spectral Blade Pact: spellblade hits drain life from enemies.
[/list]

[h2]Ranged[/h2]
[list]
[*]Hellstone Gun Mod: guns build heat; at full heat they fire faster and deal extra damage.
[*]Green Dragon Gun Mod: high crit and fire rate; full heat inflicts Cursed Inferno.
[*]Flesh Gun Mod: more fire rate and crit; full heat inflicts Ichor.
[*]Titan Gun Mod: trades attack speed and durability for heavy gun damage and critical strikes.
[*]Flesh Trigger: builds heat quickly; full-heat attacks deal extra damage that can crit.
[*]Dread Quiver: more arrow damage, crit and speed, and enemies are less likely to target you.
[/list]

[h2]Warrior Shields[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crystal Rebuttal: stores twice the damage you take for your next attack.
[*]Valadium Riposte: also stores part of the damage you deal.
[*]Lodestone Accumulator: larger storage, plus damage reduction and a life shield at low life.
[*]Piercing Barrier: high-risk storage with bonus damage and life steal at low life.
[*]Bursting Barrier: triple storage and a volatile explosion that saves you from fatal damage.
[/list]

[h2]Melee[/h2]
[list]
[*]Soul Hunter Glove: melee attacks have a chance to strike twice.
[*]Titan Bracer: extra strikes on swings and stronger melee critical hits.
[*]Dark Matter Gauntlet: build kinetic energy for bigger, faster melee attacks.
[*]Demon Blood Gauntlet: a sturdy gauntlet that can turn incoming hits into healing.
[/list]

[h2]Bard[/h2]
[list]
[*]Wireless Inspiration Headset: empowerments speed you up, and spending inspiration grants Overflowing Inspiration.
[*]Conductor's Score: symphonic bonuses plus extra damage when attacking alongside allies.
[*]Crystal Shock Absorber: stores symphonic damage to empower you and nearby allies.
[/list]

[h2]Gem Contracts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Amethyst Contract: homing Amethyst Bolts.
[*]Topaz Contract: explosive Topaz Bolts.
[*]Sapphire Contract: double-helix Sapphire Bolts.
[*]Emerald Contract: piercing Emerald Bolts that leave crystal clusters.
[*]Amber Contract: Amber Bolts that refract repeatedly.
[*]Ruby Contract: a barrage of Ruby Bolts.
[*]Diamond Contract: a giant, high-damage Diamond Bolt.
[*]Opal Contract: Opal Bolts that split on hit.
[*]Aquamarine Contract: Aquamarine Bolts that pierce multiple enemies.
[*]Crystalline Amethyst Contract: stronger homing bolts plus Crystalline Shards.
[*]Crystalline Topaz Contract: stronger explosive bolts plus Crystalline Shards.
[*]Crystalline Sapphire Contract: paired Crystalline Sapphire Bolts and shards.
[*]Crystalline Emerald Contract: longer-lasting clusters plus Crystalline Shards.
[*]Crystalline Amber Contract: Crystalline Amber Bolts that refract even more.
[*]Crystalline Ruby Contract: a longer Crystalline Ruby barrage.
[*]Crystalline Diamond Contract: a devastating giant Crystalline Diamond Bolt.
[*]Crystalline Opal Contract: splits into more crystals and releases shards.
[*]Crystalline Aquamarine Contract: stronger piercing plus Crystalline Shards.
[*]Legendary Contract: gathers nine-hued resonance, empowering summon attacks and dropping Prismatic Motes.
[*]Prismatic Mote: grants a random stack of Legendary Resonance.
[/list]
