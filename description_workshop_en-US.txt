[h1]Thorium Accessory Expansion[/h1]

An add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2909886416]Thorium[/url] that expands accessory progression for several classes. The current focus is the Dark-Healer branch, with additional magic, ranged, summon, and general accessories.

[h2]General[/h2]
[list]
[*][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Caleig/TACE/main/Accessories/Generic/BlazingLightBalloonBundle/BlazingLightBalloonBundle.png[/img] Blazing Light Balloon Bundle: quadruple jump, acceleration, fall-damage immunity, and spirit-flame dashes.
[/list]
