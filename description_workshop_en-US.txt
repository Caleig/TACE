[h1]Thorium Accessory Expansion[/h1]
An accessory add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2909886416]Thorium[/url], focused on Dark-Healer progression with magic, ranged, summon, and general accessories.

[h2]General[/h2]
[list]
[*]Blazing Light Balloon Bundle: quadruple jump and spirit-flame dash.
[/list]

[h2]Healer[/h2]
[list]
[*]Cursed Covenant: radiant power for life and Fallen Charge.
[*]Bone Covenant: less healing, higher radiant damage.
[*]Fallen Covenant: life cost and radiant critical bonuses.
[*]Heresy Covenant: radiant damage and Shadowflame amplification.
[*]Karma Covenant: healing fuels radiant damage and shared healing.
[*]Eternal Bulwark: absorb team damage; bubble at low life.
[*]Michaela's Aid: bonus healing grants radiant power.
[/list]

[h2]Magic Sheaths[/h2]
[list]
[*]Magic Sheath: mana-powered swords restore mana.
[*]Spirit Magic Sheath: more swords and magic damage.
[*]Terra Magic Sheath: homing swords, damage, and defense.
[*]Spirit Blade: magic damage and sword piercing.
[*]Blazing Scroll: sword area damage and ignition.
[*]Energy Scroll: granite energy increases damage taken.
[*]Geode Scroll: sunder increases damage taken.
[*]Holy Scroll: Holy Glare increases damage taken.
[*]Soul Scroll: sword hits drain life.
[/list]

[h2]Ranged[/h2]
[list]
[*]Hellstone Gun Modification: heat grants speed and bonus damage.
[*]Green Dragon Gun Modification: crit, speed, and Cursed Inferno.
[*]Flesh Gun Modification: speed, crit, and Ichor.
[*]Titan Gun Modification: trade speed and defense for damage.
[*]Flesh Trigger: rapid heat and critical bonus damage.
[*]Dread Quiver: arrow damage, crit, velocity, and stealth.
[/list]
